About Duojia Shen

Duojia Shen is an Advanced Practice Registered Nurse and Certified Nurse Practitioner at Essential Rheumatology in Avon, Connecticut. She works collaboratively with Dr. Wang to support comprehensive evaluation, treatment follow-up, patient education, and continuity of care.

Collaborative rheumatology care

Within the rheumatology care team, nurse practitioner visits may support symptom assessment, medication review, laboratory monitoring, treatment education, and follow-up planning. The exact scope of an individual visit depends on the patient’s needs and treatment plan.

Patient-centered follow-up

Long-term rheumatology care often requires ongoing communication and monitoring. Duojia Shen helps patients understand their care plans and works with the physician and other healthcare professionals when coordination is needed.
