Yousong Wang, MD

Board-Certified Rheumatologist & Internist

Dr. Wang has more than 20 years of clinical experience caring for adults with autoimmune, inflammatory, and joint disorders.

About Dr. Wang

Dr. Yousong Wang is a board-certified rheumatologist and internist at Essential Rheumatology in Avon, Connecticut. His approach emphasizes thoughtful evaluation, clear communication, evidence-based treatment, and continuity of care for adults living with rheumatic and musculoskeletal conditions.

Clinical care

Depending on each patient’s needs, care at Essential Rheumatology may include diagnostic assessment, medication management, joint aspiration or injection, biologic infusion, ultrasound imaging, laboratory review, ongoing disease monitoring, and coordination with other healthcare professionals.

Conditions evaluated

Dr. Wang evaluates a broad range of autoimmune, inflammatory, and musculoskeletal conditions, including rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, Sjögren’s disease, gout, psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, osteoarthritis, fibromyalgia, and related disorders.
